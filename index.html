Internal tool

Keyword research for a small printables studio.

Tripkeep Studio designs printable planners, travel journals and family games sold as digital downloads. Tripkeep Keyword Research is the private tool we use to look up search demand before we design a new product.

How the tool worksPrivacy policy

What it does

One job: tell us what people search for.

Search volume for product ideas

We enter a few seed words for a product we are thinking about, such as a road trip game or a party planner, and read the average monthly searches and trends from the Google Ads Keyword Planner.

Seasonal timing

Monthly volumes show when shoppers start looking, so we can finish a product six to ten weeks before the season.

Read-only and private

The tool only reads keyword statistics. It never creates ads, changes campaigns or spends money, and it is used by one person: the shop owner.

No customer data

It does not collect, store or share data about shoppers. The only Google account it signs in to is our own.

How it works

Four steps, run by hand.

Sign in with our own Google account

The shop owner signs in once with Google OAuth and the Google Ads API read scope. No other users can sign in.

Ask for keyword ideas or metrics

We send seed keywords to the Keyword Planner service (keyword ideas and historical metrics), usually for the United States, in English.

Read the numbers

Average monthly searches, competition level and the last twelve months of volume come back as a simple table.

Decide what to make next

We compare demand with how many similar products already exist, then design the printable. A few dozen requests a week at most.
